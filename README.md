# Shopify-Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Shopify sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand transaction performance, customer purchasing behavior, and customer retention. The interactive dashboard helps users explore sales trends, identify top-performing regions, and evaluate customer value to support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and transaction performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and repeat purchases.
- Evaluate customer retention and lifetime value.
- Identify sales trends by region, time, payment method, and product type.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Net Sales:** Total revenue generated before tax.
- **Total Quantity:** Total number of products sold.
- **Net Average Order Value:** Average revenue per transaction, excluding tax.
- **Total Customers:** Number of unique customers.
- **Single Order Customers:** Customers who placed only one order.
- **Repeat Customers:** Customers who made more than one order.
- **Lifetime Value (LTV):** Total revenue generated by a customer over time.
- **Repeat Rate:** Percentage of customers who make another purchase.
- **Purchase Frequency:** Average number of purchases per customer.

## Dashboard Features
- **Regional Analysis:** Explore sales and customer performance by province and city.
- **Sales Trends:** Analyze daily trends and customer activity by hour.
- **Payment Method Analysis:** Identify the most and least frequently used payment methods.
- **Product Analysis:** Compare revenue and order volume across product types.
- **Detailed Transaction Analysis:** Explore transaction-level data through detailed tables and drill-through functionality.
- **Interactive Filters:** Dynamically explore metrics and dashboard visualizations.
